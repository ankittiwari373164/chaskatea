Our Assam tea story

From the gardens of Assam to your cup

ChaskaTea began with a simple idea: everyone deserves a cup of chai that tastes the way chai should. Strong, aromatic and honest.

We source CTC tea from the Brahmaputra valley in Assam, where the warm, humid climate gives the leaf its famous malty strength. Every lot is tasted before we accept it, then packed fresh every week with nothing added.

  4. Sealed freshPacked weekly to lock in the aroma

Brewing guide

How to make the perfect chai

For 2 cups · about 6 minutes

  1. 1

    Boil water & spices

    1 cup water with crushed ginger or 2 elaichi. Boil 1–2 min.

    2 min
  2. 2

    Add ChaskaTea

    2 heaped teaspoons. Boil until the water turns deep red-brown.

    1 min
  3. 3

    Add milk & sugar

    1 cup full-cream milk and sugar to taste. Bring to a boil.

    1 min
  4. 4

    Simmer for strength

    Let it rise and fall 2–3 times for a truly kadak cup.

    2 min
  5. 5

    Strain & enjoy

    Pour into cups and enjoy with someone you love.

    ☕
