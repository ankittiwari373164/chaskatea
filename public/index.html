Good to know

Is delivery really free?

Yes. Every order ships free anywhere in India through our courier partners, and you get a tracking link by SMS and email.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, cash on delivery is available. Paying online with UPI, cards or netbanking through Razorpay gives you an extra saving on every pack.

How fast will my order arrive?

Most orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours and arrive in 3–7 working days depending on your pin code. Track any order from the Track page.

Does ChaskaTea contain any added flavour or colour?

No. It is 100% natural Assam CTC leaf with nothing added.

How do I brew the perfect cup?

Boil water and milk, add one heaped teaspoon per cup, simmer for around 3 minutes, add sugar to taste and strain. Ginger or cardamom makes it even better.
