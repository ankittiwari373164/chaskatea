How to Store Tea Leaves So They Stay Fresh for Months

By ChaskaTea · 10 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Tea is a dried leaf, and like spices it slowly loses its aroma once the pack is opened. Store it well and the last cup tastes as good as the first.

The four enemies of fresh tea

  • Air: oxygen slowly fades aroma. Keep the container tightly closed.
  • Moisture: damp tea loses flavour and can spoil. Always use a dry spoon.
  • Light: sunlight breaks down flavour compounds. Use an opaque container.
  • Strong smells: tea absorbs odours easily. Keep it away from masalas, onions and garlic.

Best way to store your tea

  1. Transfer tea to a clean, dry, airtight steel or opaque glass container.
  2. Keep the rest in the original pack, tightly folded and clipped.
  3. Store in a cool, dark cupboard away from the stove.
  4. Don't keep tea in the fridge, where moisture condenses each time you take it out.
