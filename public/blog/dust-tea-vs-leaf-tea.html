Dust Tea vs Leaf Tea: Which Tea is Best for Kadak Chai?

By ChaskaTea · 20 Oct 2026 · 4 min read

Walk into any kirana store and you will see tea labelled leaf, granules or dust. The right choice depends on how you like your chai and how you make it.

Dust tea

Dust tea is the finest grade of CTC tea. Because the particles are so small, it brews very quickly and gives a deep colour and strong taste. It is ideal for tea stalls, hotels, offices and big families who make chai in large quantities, and for anyone who likes it really kadak.

Try: ChaskaTea Supreme Dust, S8 and S9.

Granule (CTC) tea

Granular CTC is the everyday choice in most Indian homes. It gives a balanced cup: good colour, full body and rich aroma, without being harsh.

Try: ChaskaTea Classic for everyday kadak chai, or Premium for a smoother, more aromatic cup.

Quick guide

  • Strong morning chai at home: Classic
  • Masala or elaichi chai with great aroma: Premium
  • Tea stall, restaurant or very strong chai: Supreme
  • Can't decide? A Classic + Premium combo lets you try both and save.
