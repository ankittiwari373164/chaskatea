Assam Tea Benefits: Why a Daily Cup of Chai is Good for You

By ChaskaTea · 05 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Assam tea is a black tea, made from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant grown in Assam. Alongside its bold taste, a cup of natural Assam tea brings some genuine everyday benefits when enjoyed in moderation.

1. Natural antioxidants

Black tea naturally contains polyphenols, including theaflavins and thearubigins, which act as antioxidants. Choosing natural tea with no artificial colour, like ChaskaTea, means you get the tea as it is.

2. Gentle, steady energy

A cup of black tea contains less caffeine than a cup of coffee. Tea also contains L-theanine, an amino acid associated with calm focus, so many people find tea gives a smoother lift.

3. A habit that supports hydration

Tea is mostly water, and a few cups a day contribute to your daily fluid intake.

4. A moment to pause

Some of chai's biggest benefits are social: a few minutes of rest, a conversation with family, a break from the screen.

How to enjoy chai in a healthier way

  • Use less sugar, or try jaggery in small amounts.
  • Add ginger, tulsi or cardamom for flavour without extra sugar.
  • Stick to 2–4 cups a day and avoid very late-night chai if caffeine affects your sleep.
  • Choose natural tea with no artificial colour.

This article is for general information and is not medical advice. If you have a health condition or are pregnant, ask your doctor about caffeine.
