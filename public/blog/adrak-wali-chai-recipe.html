Adrak Wali Chai Recipe: Ginger Tea for Winter and Monsoon

By ChaskaTea · 28 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Nothing beats a hot cup of adrak wali chai on a rainy evening or a cold winter morning. Fresh ginger adds warmth and a gentle spice that makes it a favourite with pakoras and samosas.

Ingredients for 2 cups

  • 1 cup water and 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons ChaskaTea Classic
  • 1.5 inch fresh ginger, grated or crushed
  • Sugar to taste

Method

  1. Boil the water with the crushed ginger for 2 minutes until it smells strong.
  2. Add the tea and boil for 1 minute.
  3. Add milk and sugar, bring to a boil, then simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Strain and serve hot.

Why does milk sometimes curdle in ginger tea?

Fresh ginger contains an enzyme that can curdle milk. To avoid it, always boil the ginger in water before adding the milk, and bring the milk to a full boil after adding it. Boiling deactivates the enzyme.

Variations

  • Adrak-elaichi chai: add 2 crushed cardamom pods with the ginger.
  • Tulsi-adrak chai: add 5–6 tulsi leaves for a soothing herbal note.
  • Extra kadak: use ChaskaTea Supreme for a darker, stronger cup.
