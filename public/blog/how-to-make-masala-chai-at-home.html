How to Make Perfect Masala Chai at Home (Step-by-Step Recipe)

By ChaskaTea · 18 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

A good cup of masala chai is the first thing many of us reach for in the morning and the last comfort of a long evening. It looks simple, but the difference between an ordinary cup and a truly kadak, aromatic one comes down to three things: the tea you use, the order you add ingredients, and how long you let it boil.

Ingredients for 2 cups

  • 1 cup water (240 ml)
  • 1 cup full-cream milk (240 ml)
  • 2 heaped teaspoons ChaskaTea Premium or Classic CTC tea
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, crushed
  • 2 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 2 cloves and a small piece of cinnamon (optional)
  • 2–3 teaspoons sugar, to taste

Method

  1. Boil the spices first. Add water, ginger, cardamom and the other spices to a saucepan and boil for 1–2 minutes. This releases their oils before the tea goes in.
  2. Add the tea. Add the CTC tea and let it boil for about 1 minute until the water turns deep red-brown.
  3. Add milk and sugar. Pour in the milk and sugar and bring it back to a rolling boil.
  4. Simmer for colour and strength. Lower the heat and simmer for 2–3 minutes. For extra kadak chai, let it rise and fall two or three times.
  5. Strain and serve. Strain into cups and serve hot.

Tips for the perfect cup

  • Use CTC tea. Assam CTC brews fast and gives the strong colour and body that stands up to milk and spices.
  • Crush, don't chop. Crushed ginger and cardamom give far more flavour than sliced.
  • Don't over-boil. More than 5–6 minutes of boiling makes chai bitter as tannins take over.
  • Full-cream milk gives the creamiest cup. With toned milk, reduce the water slightly.
